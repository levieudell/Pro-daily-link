COMMON QUESTIONS

Is Pro Daily Link a Procore replacement?

No. Procore is a full construction management platform covering drawings, RFIs, submittals, and financials. Pro Daily Link focuses on field daily reports, estimate-vs-actual production, and catching extra work. Many teams use both.

How does pricing compare?

Procore uses quote-based pricing that scales with your projects and products, typically starting in the hundreds per month. Pro Daily Link is a flat company price from $99 per month, with every plan including the whole crew up to its user limit.

Which is faster to roll out to a crew?

Pro Daily Link. It is one workflow—start the day, report what happened, verify production—and it is self-guided with templates. A broad platform takes more setup and more decisions before the first daily is submitted.

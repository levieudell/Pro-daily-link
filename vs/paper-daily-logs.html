COMMON QUESTIONS

What is wrong with paper daily logs?

Nothing, until something goes wrong. Paper gets lost, sits in trucks, is hard to read, and makes the office re-type everything. Photos live in personal phones with no link to the report. When a dispute comes up months later, the record is hard to find.

Do field crews need smartphones to use Pro Daily Link?

Yes, a basic smartphone is needed. The field screens are built glove-friendly, with voice notes, so the report takes minutes, not a paperwork session at the truck.

What happens if there is no signal on site?

Typed and voice-noted drafts stay saved on the phone and can be finished and submitted when signal returns. Photos wait for a connection instead of being lost.

Is it expensive compared to paper?

Paper feels free until you count the hours spent chasing, re-typing, and reconstructing records. Pro Daily Link starts at $99 per month flat for the whole company, with a 14-day free trial and no credit card.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Made to fit your process—not replace everything overnight.

Does Pro Daily Link replace my accounting software?

No. The first release produces verified reports and exports that can feed your existing billing and payroll processes.

Can field employees see project costs?

No. Field access is scoped to assigned work, dailies, photos, and their own workflow. Financial data stays on the office side.

What if the crew did work that was not estimated?

They can add custom work. The office receives an exception and decides whether to map it, treat it as T&M, open a potential change, or mark it non-billable.

Can Spanish-speaking crews use it?

Yes. Original Spanish notes can be preserved while the office receives English translation and structured production details.

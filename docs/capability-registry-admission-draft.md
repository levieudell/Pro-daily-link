## Finite account responses and identity

Only atomic current-user/bootstrap, login, owner directory and user-update responses use finite DTOs. Unknown private fields, password/setup/reset/session/proof/profile markers and arbitrary nested values are never spread into responses. Current preferences are explicitly shaped; the owner directory omits private preferences and photos. Foreman keeps its internal accessRole with the existing field presentation in current-user responses. Inactive accounts have no effective actions. Stored individual grants and effective typed capabilities are separate fields; the server never writes effective reductions back into grants. Baseline account impacts are actual feature/grant intersections, not role ceilings.

Shared atomic authentication re-resolves the live session/account before business and assistant authority. Duplicate hashed sessions, globally Number-aligned user aliases, foreign account/session bindings, nested primitive authority/grant fields, nonprimitive crew evidence, and ambiguous or noncanonical tenant team/project identity evidence fail closed before business reads. This protects legacy Number-based crew/project projections as well as metadata. Such diagnostics require reconciliation before a future policy preview activation.

Read-only account-access, directory and registry responses, and renewed bootstrap, fence fresh revision/session/account before delivery. User edits keep original owner lockout and input/audit behavior, with finite responses and an outer CAS. Broad /api/state and scoped project/team/customer navigation remain unavailable; this increment does not claim complete app bootstrap or the full Roles editor.

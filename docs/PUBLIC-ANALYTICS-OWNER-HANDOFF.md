## What is ready

The implementation is OFF (`public-analytics-config.js`), with no measurement ID. It loads only on Home, About, Field Notes index, Signup, Privacy and Terms. Authentication, guest links, support, password flows, private workspace and platform pages have no tracker. Visible company cookies suppress tracking even on public pages. No user IDs, form values, signup success details, blog article titles, project names or customer data are sent. Signup is a public page view only, not an account conversion event.

Basic consent: no Google script or consent ping until Allow analytics. Decline makes no Google request. Preferences are separate from the signup terms checkbox. Withdrawal disables collection, removes GA cookies across applicable domains, and reloads to unload the tag. Other tabs reload when the preference changes. GPC and Do Not Track suppress collection. Local/preview hosts are excluded. No campaign storage is written before consent. Preferences persist locally until changed; GA cookies have a 30-day lifetime without renewal.

Manual pageviews use fixed page titles, sanitized locations and referrer origins. Duplicate script installation and identical consecutive pageviews are prevented. Public history navigation is handled; a transition to a private/unknown route disables the tag. Unknown queries, unreviewed campaign values, tokens, email parameters, ad click IDs and unknown fragments suppress the entire visit. Only reviewed utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign values can be collected and carried into same-origin signup links. Plan selection is preserved but omitted from the measured URL. An arbitrary campaign string can contain personal information even when it matches a simple character pattern, so a fixed allowlist is required.

## Proposed privacy policy text (requires approval)

“With your optional analytics consent, public website pages and the signup page use Google Analytics to measure visits, pages viewed, referral sources, approved campaign labels, and approximate location derived by Google from network information. Google also receives browser/device information and uses analytics cookies. We exclude private workspaces and signup form entries from analytics. Analytics is not used for advertising personalization. You can decline or withdraw consent through Analytics preferences on eligible public pages; we also honor Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. Declining does not prevent signup or use of the service. Google processes analytics information under its own privacy terms. These measurements cover consenting visits after activation and may omit blocked, opted-out, internal or otherwise excluded visits.”

Add Google Analytics to the service-provider list and distinguish approximate public-site analytics location from device location used by requested app features. ## Viewing reports

Realtime checks new collection; Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition shows referrals and campaign source/medium; Engagement → Pages and screens shows measured pages; User attributes → Demographic details provides approximate location subject to availability/thresholds. Reports may take time to populate. To give someone report-only access, owner uses Admin → Property access management → Add users → **Viewer**, after approving their exact Google email. No new access has been granted by this task. No retrospective data is created. “Users” is a browser/device-based metric and does not prove unique humans; counts omit non-consenting/excluded traffic.

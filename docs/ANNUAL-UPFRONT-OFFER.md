# First-year upfront annual offer

Only a new signup can opt in, with explicit annual billing and upfront/renewal consent. First-year pretax amounts are Starter891USD, Growth1791USD, Pro3591USD. Standard annual prices990/1990/3990 remain the recurring prices; a single10% coupon with duration=once discounts only the first invoice. Regular annual renewals apply automatically until cancelled. No campaign expiry or customer cap is assumed.

Normal monthly/annual trial signup remains unchanged. Founder membership and other discounts cannot stack. Offer selection is durable server-owned signup data, not a checkout request flag. Upfront signups start Incomplete with no trial; provider-active status alone cannot grant access without matching paid invoice evidence. A consumed offer is never granted again after cancellation/resubscription. Exact Checkout parameters, coupon and provider mode remain bound to the persisted idempotent attempt.

Configuration: STRIPE_FIRST_YEAR_ANNUAL_COUPON must identify a valid10% duration=once coupon in the same mode, without amount_off, redemption cap/deadline or product restriction. Existing annual prices are validated for USD/year/amount. Neither live nor connected sandbox had a coupon during read-only inspection; no coupon or billing configuration was created. Without this setting the optional choice stays hidden and opted-in requests fail closed; normal signup works. Never put keys/coupon configuration in browser assets.

Before enabling live: separately approve creation of one live10% once-only coupon (no promotion code/cap/expiry) and setting this one environment variable to its ID. Do not change existing prices or customer subscriptions. Approve sandbox coupon configuration separately if provider acceptance is requested. Exercise Checkout display, first invoice, annual renewal, failure and cancellation in an isolated sandbox; current tests are provider-mocked HTTP acceptance and do not establish real signed provider delivery.

Containment: remove the new coupon setting to hide/block new offer signup/Checkout. Do not delete accepted offer state or exact pending attempts, cancel paid subscriptions, alter founder schedules, reset trial dates, or drop payment/invoice verification. Existing paid offers must retain webhook handling and renewal validation. PDL_CHECKOUT_DISABLED blocks all new Checkouts if broader containment is needed. No SQL migration; additive company annualUpfront record.

Validation: annual-upfront.test.js covers three first invoice/renewal amounts, paid entitlements, duplicate events, cancellation without a repeat discount, coupon/currency/plan mismatches, founder/discount non-stacking, explicit consent, concurrent/lost-response Checkout retries and normal trial preservation. Subscription repair, founder and onboarding suites remain required. Independent review and exact-head CI precede release.

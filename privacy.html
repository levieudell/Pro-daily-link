Pro Daily Link

Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

This policy explains how Pro Daily Link handles information when you use the service. “You” means the company that uses Pro Daily Link and the people who use it for that company.

Information we collect

We collect account and company details, user roles, project and customer records, schedules, daily reports, labor entries, production quantities, time cards, uploaded photos and documents, support communications, subscription information, and technical security logs. Payment-card information is processed by Stripe and is not stored by Pro Daily Link.

How information is used

We use information to provide and secure the service, isolate company workspaces, process requested features, support users, maintain backups, improve workflows, communicate important account information, and comply with law.

Location, photos, and voice

Device location is used when a user chooses a location-based feature such as weather. Optional public-site analytics can also provide approximate location derived by Google from network information, as described below. Photos may include capture metadata supplied by the device. Voice or typed field notes may be processed to transcribe, translate, or organize a suggested result. Review that result before you submit it.

Optional public website analytics

When enabled and with your optional consent, public website pages and the signup page use Google Analytics to measure visits, pages viewed, referral sources, approved campaign labels, and approximate location. Google receives browser and device information and uses analytics cookies. Private workspaces and signup form entries are excluded. We do not use this analytics collection for advertising personalization. Our existing Google Ads link can make non-personalized measurement available in Google Ads.

You can decline or withdraw consent through Analytics preferences on eligible public pages. We also honor Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. Declining does not prevent signup or use of the service. Our analytics cookies are limited to 30 days without renewal. Reports cover consenting visits after activation and may omit blocked, opted-out, internal or otherwise excluded visits. Learn how Google collects and processes information from sites that use its services.

Service providers

We use service providers for hosting, database and file storage, payments, email, artificial intelligence, weather, error monitoring, optional public website analytics, and website typefaces. The providers are Render for hosting, Supabase for the database and file storage, Stripe for payments, Resend for email, OpenAI for artificial intelligence, Open-Meteo for weather, Sentry for error monitoring, and Google Analytics for optional public website analytics when enabled. The public site and the app also load typefaces from Google Fonts. Each provider receives only the information needed for its function and operates under its own terms.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with authorized users of your company, with the service providers above, or with authorities when the law requires it. Time-limited support access is logged and is used only for a documented support purpose.

Security and retention

We use tenant boundaries, role-based permissions, encrypted connections, private storage, credential hashing, backups, logging, and access controls. No system is completely secure. After the service ends, you have 30 days to export your data. After those 30 days, we delete that data in the ordinary course. Backups age out on their normal schedule. We may keep copies needed for security, billing, dispute resolution, or the law.

Customer responsibilities and choices

Company account owners control users and project access. Requests to access, correct, export, or delete information may be sent to the contact below.

Children

Pro Daily Link is a business service and is not intended for children under 13.

Policy changes

Material changes will be posted with a new effective date. When the law requires notice, we will send it.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to support@prodailylink.com.
